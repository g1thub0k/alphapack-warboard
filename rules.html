Clan promotion & demotion rules

Our role system runs on an automated tracker that checks everyone’s war performance every week. Here’s exactly how it works, so there are no surprises.

Promotions

  • Member → Elder: Complete all 16/16 war attacks over a full 4-week season, and average at least 2,000 Fame each of those weeks.
  • Member → Elder (performance boost — new members only): If you average 800+ medals/day (or 3,200+/week) in war for 2 straight weeks as a new member, you can fast-track to Elder in just 2 weeks instead of 4.
  • Elder → Co-Leader: 3+ months in the clan, and averaging 700+ medals per war day over the last 4-week season.

Demotions

  • Co-Leader → Elder: Missing 3 or more war attacks and falling below 2,500 Fame in that same week, with no advance notice.
  • Elder → Member: Missing 3 or more war attacks and falling below 2,200 Fame that week, no advance notice — or landing in the bottom 10% of the clan in weekly medals and falling below the 2,200 Fame floor that week.

Member removal flag

Missing all 16 war attacks in a week (the entire week) with no advance notice will flag you for removal from the clan. One missed week happens — just give us a heads up beforehand.

The notice rule

Life happens — work, school, travel. If you let us know beforehand that you’ll miss attacks, it won’t count against you. But: only 1 notice is honored per rolling 4 weeks. If you need an excuse most weeks, it stops counting as an exception — so use it when you really need it.

Why all this?

We’re pushing from Legendary 2 to Legendary 1. That takes consistency from everyone. These rules are the same for every member, checked automatically and fairly every week — no favorites, no guessing.
