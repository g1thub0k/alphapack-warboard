Clan rules

Fifty people. Four decks. Every war day. The Action column is the referee: red tile, yellow tile, orange tiles, or a mix.

How to read a week

16/16
All four decks, Thursday through Sunday. Needed for the eight-week streak.
Blank day
Zero decks on a day that already ended. Leftover decks are not a blank day.
0/4
No attacks on all four war days.
Notice
Tell us before or during the week. We tick Pending Notice. Missed days that week are excused (not low Fame on days you did attack). One notice every four weeks.

A noticed week is not 16/16. It cannot be yellow or a kick for missed days. It can still be orange.

What you see under Action

member saved from a kick once (Red Card)   missed days   showed up every day, fame too low   OK clean   Demote drop one role   Kick seat is free

No words on the tiles. Two orange tiles means two orange weeks. Tap a tile to see the reason.

Stacking: elders and co-leaders can hold Yellow and Orange at once (e.g. Yellow + Orange 2). Members don't get Yellow — they can hold Red and Orange at once instead (e.g. Red + Orange 2).

Yellow — missed days

Elders and co-leaders. One yellow tile. Role stays when it is issued.

What happenedResult
Two blank days, no notice Yellow when the second day ends (usually Saturday)
One blank day and under the fame floor Yellow Monday. Floor is 2,500 co-leader / 2,200 elder. If you have oranges, one comes off
One blank day, fame at or over the floor No yellow. If you have oranges, one comes off
Full 0/4 after eight straight 16/16 weeks Yellow Monday — the streak protects the role once
Full 0/4 with fewer than eight 16/16 weeks Demote Monday (or Kick if you are a member)
Already yellow, then two blank days Demote when the second day ends

Co-leader drops to elder. Elder drops to member. Two straight 16/16 weeks clear Yellow. Orange still moves on its own those weeks (see Elder & Co-Leader Performance Rules below).

15/16, 14/16, 13/16 are not yellow if you played every day. 12/16 is yellow only when one of those days was actually zero — unless fame is under the floor, in which case one blank day is yellow anyway.

Elder & Co-Leader Performance Rules

Low Output Despite Full Attendance: Orange Card

If you attack on every war day but finish the week below your role's Fame floor, you earn an Orange Card.

  • Fame floors: Elders 2200, Co-Leaders 2500.
  • Orange Cards build up week over week. Each week you attack every day and finish under your floor adds one.
  • 4 Orange weeks in a row results in demotion: Elders drop to Member, Co-Leaders drop to Elder.
  • Any week you clear your floor with full attendance reduces your Orange count by one. A week with exactly 1 missed day also reduces it by one, but it can never add to it.
  • A Notice does not protect against Orange. Notice only excuses missed days, not low Fame on days you did attack.
  • Orange and Yellow Cards are tracked separately, so you can hold both at the same time.

Kick — the seat is free

New members and members (not elder / co-leader):

  • Two blank days, no notice → out when the second day ends (usually Saturday)
  • A full 0/4 week, no notice → out Monday

Either kick can be avoided once by the Red Card exemption — see Member Attendance & Performance below.

Elders and co-leaders are not kicked on the first collapse. They drop a role first. After elder → member, the kick rule applies.

Eight straight 16/16 weeks is what turns one 0/4 into a yellow. Contribution total and months in the clan do not.

Member Attendance & Performance Rules (updated)

Missed Attacks

A Member who misses 2 or more attack days in a war week is normally removed from the clan.

Performance Exemption — Red Card

If your average Medals/Day over your last 4 completed war weeks is 600 or higher, you’ll be spared from that kick — once. Being saved this way puts you on a Red Card for the next 4 weeks.

  • While on a Red Card, a second war week with 2+ missed days results in immediate removal — no exceptions, regardless of how strong your average still is.
  • The Red Card clears automatically after 4 weeks pass without another 2+-missed-days week.
  • A single missed day (not two) during those 4 weeks is fine and won’t restart the clock.

Low Output Despite Full Attendance — Orange Card

Even if you attack every day, averaging below 2000 Fame for the week earns an Orange Card.

  • Orange Cards build up week over week if your Fame stays under 2000.
  • 4 Orange weeks in a row results in removal.
  • Any week you clear 2000 Fame with full attendance reduces your Orange count by one. A week with exactly 1 missed day also reduces it by one, but it can never add to it.

Promotions

Monday only. No promotion the same week you are demoted or kicked.

  • Member → Elder: 16/16 each week for four straight weeks, and at least 2,000 fame each of those weeks.
  • Fast track (first two weeks in the clan only): two straight weeks of 16/16 and 3,200 fame each week.
  • Elder → Co-leader: 3+ months in the clan, and averaging 700+ medals per war day over the last 4-week season.

When the board updates

Saturday / Sunday Blank-day yellows, Red Cards, mid-week demotes, kicks. Days not started yet are not misses. Monday Fame, orange add/wipe, 0/4, promotions.

Why

World ranking needs a full boat every day. Same rules for everyone. The tracker decides; this page is how to read it.
